## Resources

Essential links, official guidelines, and tools for FIRST LEGO League participants and supporters:

- **[FIRST LEGO League Challenge](https://www.firstlegoleague.org/challenge):** Explore current season challenge guidelines, robot game updates, and competition details.
- **[Score Calculator](https://flltournament.com/Scoresheet.aspx):** Interactive online scoring sheets to practice routines, track scores, and evaluate strategy runs.
- **[FIRST Inspires Official Portal](https://www.firstinspires.org/robotics/fll):** Learn more about the FIRST ethos, Core Values, scholarships, and STEM youth programs worldwide.
- **[Robot Game Updates & Rules](https://www.firstlegoleague.org/season):** Stay up to date with the latest official rule clarifications, field setup adjustments, and Q&A updates.

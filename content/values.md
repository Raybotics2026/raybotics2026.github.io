## Our Core Values

FIRST LEGO League emphasizes friendly competition and mutual gain through the FIRST Core Values. These principles guide our team during every build, test, and presentation:

- **Discovery:** We explore new skills and ideas, continuously learning from our challenges and tests.
- **Innovation:** We use creativity, ingenuity, and persistence to design original solutions.
- **Impact:** We apply what we learn to make a positive, lasting difference in our world.
- **Inclusion:** We respect each other, listen to diverse perspectives, and celebrate individual strengths.
- **Teamwork:** We are stronger and more capable when we collaborate and support each other.
- **Fun:** We enjoy the learning journey, celebrate milestones, and find joy in the process!

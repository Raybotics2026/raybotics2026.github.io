Testing and Debugging

We use a systematic approach to testing:

  • Unit testing individual functions
  • Integration testing with multiple motors and sensors
  • Full mission testing on the practice field
  • Stress testing under competition conditions
  • Debugging with data logging and sensor output monitoring

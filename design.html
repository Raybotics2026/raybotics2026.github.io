Robot Design

The design of our robot is a carefully planned combination of mechanical engineering, innovative thinking, and practical problem-solving. Our design process began with understanding the challenges of the FLL competition and identifying the key missions our robot needed to accomplish.

Design Philosophy

Our robot design prioritizes:

  • Reliability: Our robot is built to consistently complete missions without failure
  • Modularity: Different attachments can be quickly swapped for different missions
  • Simplicity: Fewer moving parts mean fewer things can go wrong
  • Efficiency: Our robot completes tasks in the shortest time possible
  • Innovation: Creative solutions to unique challenges

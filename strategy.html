Robot Strategy

Winning at First LEGO League is not just about having a great robot—it's about having a winning strategy. Our team spent significant time analyzing the challenge, identifying the highest-scoring missions, and planning our approach.

Strategic Overview

Our strategy focuses on:

  • Mission Prioritization: Focusing on high-scoring missions with the best success rate
  • Time Management: Maximizing the use of our 2.5-minute match time
  • Risk Assessment: Balancing ambitious missions with reliable backups
  • Team Coordination: Clear communication and defined roles during competition
  • Continuous Improvement: Testing and refining our approach throughout the season

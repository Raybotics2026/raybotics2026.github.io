Welcome to Raybotics 2026

Welcome to the official website of Raybotics 2026, a First LEGO League (FLL) team dedicated to innovation, teamwork, and discovery. Our mission is to inspire and empower young people to become problem-solvers and creative thinkers through robotics and STEM learning.

About Our Team

Raybotics 2026 is a competitive FLL team comprised of talented young engineers, programmers, and researchers. We work together to design and build innovative robots, develop solutions to real-world problems, and share our knowledge with the community.

Our Core Values

  • Discovery: We embrace learning and exploration in all that we do.
  • Teamwork: Together we accomplish more than we could individually.
  • Fun: We enjoy the journey and find joy in our challenges.
  • Inclusion: We welcome and value every team member.
  • Innovation: We think differently and create new solutions.

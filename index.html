About Our Team

Raybotics 2026 is a competitive FIRST LEGO League team comprised of enthusiastic young engineers, programmers, and problem solvers.

Our mission is to inspire and empower team members to explore STEM concepts, master robotics challenges, and develop innovative solutions to real-world problems. Together, we learn how to research thoroughly, design mechanically sound attachments, code autonomous robot routines, and share our enthusiasm with our community.

Our Core Values

FIRST LEGO League emphasizes friendly competition and mutual gain through the FIRST Core Values. These principles guide our team during every build, test, and presentation:

  • Discovery: We explore new skills and ideas, continuously learning from our challenges and tests.
  • Innovation: We use creativity, ingenuity, and persistence to design original solutions.
  • Impact: We apply what we learn to make a positive, lasting difference in our world.
  • Inclusion: We respect each other, listen to diverse perspectives, and celebrate individual strengths.
  • Teamwork: We are stronger and more capable when we collaborate and support each other.
  • Fun: We enjoy the learning journey, celebrate milestones, and find joy in the process!

Robot Design

Our robot design process is centered on mechanical reliability, modularity, and rapid attachment swapping in the launch area.

Design Philosophy

  • Reliability: Consistent mission runs through rigid framing and balanced center of gravity.
  • Modularity: Passive alignment jigs and tool-less quick-release attachments.
  • Simplicity: Fewer moving parts to minimize potential mechanical failure points.
  • Precision: Integrating high-accuracy motors with gyro sensors and dual color sensors.

Robot Strategy

A high-scoring Robot Game performance requires clear prioritization, time management, and contingency preparation during the 2.5-minute match window.

Strategic Priorities

  • Mission Prioritization: We group missions geographically across the mat, combining multiple objectives into single launch runs.
  • 2.5-Minute Match Management: Each team member has a designated role to ensure swift attachment swaps.
  • Risk Management & Baseline Points: We establish safe, high-reliability baseline runs first.
  • Contingency Procedures: Fallback routines for unexpected field conditions.

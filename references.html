Citation Guidelines Used

All citations follow the APA (American Psychological Association) citation format, which includes:

  • Author(s): Last name, First initial(s)
  • Publication Year: In parentheses
  • Title: Article or book title in sentence case
  • Source: Journal name, website, or publisher
  • URL/DOI: When applicable
